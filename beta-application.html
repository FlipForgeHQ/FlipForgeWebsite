What testers should expect

Test the decision layer—not just the interface.

AccessAuthenticated web workspace

Testing is focused on the hosted customer product through a modern browser.

EvidenceSome cases should fail closed

Missing identity or weak evidence should not be turned into false confidence just to produce an answer.

FeedbackSpecific beats general

The most useful report names the card, expected behavior, actual behavior, and why the difference matters.

OutcomesLater review matters

Where possible, revisit saved decisions so FlipForge can compare its original reasoning with later evidence.

BoundaryNo transaction or grade guarantee

Beta guidance is decision support. It does not buy cards, guarantee returns, or guarantee a future PSA grade.
