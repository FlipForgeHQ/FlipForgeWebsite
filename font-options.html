Font options for paragraph readability.

This page compares cleaner body fonts while keeping the premium FlipForge display style for brand headings. The goal is simple: paragraphs should be easy to read, while the logo and headings still feel sharp and high-end.

Inter

Inter is clean, modern, highly readable, and safe for a professional SaaS website. This is the strongest default choice if you want the site to feel legitimate immediately.
