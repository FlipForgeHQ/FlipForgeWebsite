The Product

A decision is only as strong as the evidence behind it.

FlipForge is the intelligence layer between finding a card and deciding what to do next. It resolves the exact identity, tests the evidence, evaluates the economics, keeps uncertainty visible, and preserves the reason trail.

Identity firstEvidence before priceRisk stays visibleExplainable outcomesNo transaction authority

02 · EvidenceKnow what truly matches.Accepted exact sales stay separate from context, review-only records, and rejects.
03 · EconomicsKnow what the setup costs.Ask, supported value, acquisition cost, grading cost, and scenario outcomes.
04 · RiskKnow what could break the thesis.Confidence, liquidity, downside, evidence weakness, and unresolved assumptions.
05 · DecisionKnow why.BUY CANDIDATE, WATCH, VERIFY, PASS, or grading guidance with traceback.
Identity First

One changed field can mean a different card market.

FlipForge treats identity as a gate, not a suggestion. A convincing price cannot repair a wrong parallel, wrong grade, or unresolved card number.

Evidence Trust

A completed sale is not automatically a usable comp.

FlipForge records why a record was accepted, held for review, or rejected. Asking-price discovery stays separate from completed-sale support.

Accepted evidenceExact-card completed sales can support value when identity and provenance clear the rules.
Review-only contextIncomplete or ambiguous records remain visible without silently influencing the supported result.
Explicit exclusionsWrong grades, parallels, duplicates, stale records, and unresolved mismatches stay out.
Traceable provenanceThe decision can show what entered the evidence set and why.
