The Product

Decision intelligence built around the questions collectors actually face.

FlipForge is a Windows private-beta platform that turns authorized listings, user evidence, exact-card controls, market support, grading scenarios, and risk into an explainable candidate state.

Identity First

The recommendation is only as reliable as the card behind it.

Year, set, card number, parallel, variation, grader, and grade are kept separate. Conflicts trigger review or rejection before evidence affects value.

Exact matching

Base, refractor, numbered parallel, variation, raw, and graded versions are not treated as interchangeable.

Conflict control

Ambiguous or contradictory records are blocked instead of silently averaged.

Evidence Trust

Completed sales, asking prices, and mismatches have different jobs.

FlipForge records provenance and classifies evidence as accepted, review-only, or rejected. The final traceback explains what influenced the outcome.

Accepted

Strong exact-card completed sales support value.

Review

Incomplete records and asking prices provide context.

Rejected

Wrong grades, parallels, duplicates, and stale records stay out.
