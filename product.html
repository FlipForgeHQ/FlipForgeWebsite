What FlipForge Does

Turn a card and its market data into an explainable decision.

The product is built around three jobs: identify the exact card, decide what evidence is usable, and explain the resulting guidance without hiding uncertainty.

01

03

Decide

What It Evaluates

The decision comes from layers, not one magic number.

Each layer answers a different question. Weakness in one layer stays visible instead of being averaged away.

Identity

Is this actually the same card, parallel, variation, grader, and grade?

Evidence

Which completed sales or market records deserve to influence the evaluation?

Economics

What do purchase cost, grading scenarios, fees, and downside do to the setup?

Market Risk

How strong are confidence, liquidity, market depth, and unresolved uncertainty?

Explore Deeper

Open the part of the product you want to understand.

The detail is still here, but you do not have to absorb all of it at once.

01Identity + EvidenceWhy the comp is or is not allowed to count

FlipForge keeps seller claims separate from confirmed card attributes. Evidence can be accepted, held for review, or rejected so a wrong parallel, grade, duplicate, or weak record does not quietly become confidence.

02Grading EconomicsWhy the PSA 10 price is not the expected return

Raw value, grading cost, multiple grade outcomes, break-even points, and downside belong in the same scenario. FlipForge is designed to model the range rather than anchor to one best-case grade.

03Decision TracebackWhy the result changed and what to verify next

The outcome is not meant to be a black box. FlipForge preserves the evidence, exclusions, uncertainty, and reasoning behind the guidance so you can see what caused it and what could change it.
