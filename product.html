1 · Exact identity

One changed field can mean a different card market.

You should not have to wonder whether a similar-looking result silently became “close enough.” FlipForge treats identity as a gate. When the parallel, grade, variation, or card number is decision-critical and unresolved, the workflow stops instead of inventing certainty.

Exact matchingBase, refractor, numbered parallels, variations, raw, and graded lanes remain distinct.
Explicit ambiguityWhen several identities are plausible, the customer chooses instead of the system silently auto-picking the first match.
Card-number normalizationEquivalent notations can resolve to one identity while missing decision-critical numbers still fail closed.
Photo-assisted reviewVisual identification can help populate the target without granting recommendation authority.

2 · Evidence trust

A real sale can still be the wrong comp for your decision.

FlipForge records why a record was accepted, held for review, or rejected. A price is not made safer by averaging it with evidence from a different parallel, grade, duplicate source, lot, or unresolved identity.

Accepted evidenceExact-card completed sales can support the decision when identity and provenance clear the rules.
Review-only contextIncomplete records can stay visible without silently influencing supported value.
Explicit exclusionsWrong grades, parallels, duplicates, stale records, and unresolved mismatches stay out.
Traceable provenanceYou can see what entered the evidence set and why.
