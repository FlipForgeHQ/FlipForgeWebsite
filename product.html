The Product

More than a price. An intelligence system for the decision.

FlipForge connects exact-card identity, market evidence, grading economics, confidence, liquidity, and risk—then shows you why the result deserves your attention.

Defining Intelligence

The technology that makes FlipForge more than a price guide.

Each intelligence layer answers a different question. Together, they turn noisy card-market data into an explainable decision.

01Confidence Engine

ForgeScore™

Know how much confidence the evidence has earned.

ForgeScore makes confidence visible inside the evaluation and opportunity workflow. Instead of hiding uncertainty behind a single price, FlipForge surfaces how strong the underlying case is and lets high- and low-confidence candidates stand apart.

Confidence you can inspect—not just a number you are asked to trust.
02Opportunity Intelligence

ForgeSignal™

Surface opportunities worth investigating.

The ForgeSignal Opportunity Feed ranks candidates while preserving evidence gates, duplicate filtering, value-gap context, profit and ROI screens, and direct handoff into deeper review. It finds candidates; it does not auto-buy them.

Spend your attention where the setup deserves a closer look.
03Identity Intelligence

Exact-Card Identity

Value the card you actually have.

Year, set, card number, parallel, variation, grader, and grade remain separate. Conflicts are reviewed or blocked before mismatched evidence can distort the decision.

A convincing comp cannot repair the wrong identity.
04Evidence Intelligence

Evidence Trust

A comp has to earn the right to count.

Evidence can be accepted, held for review, or rejected. Wrong grades, parallels, duplicates, weak records, and context-only prices stay visible instead of being silently blended into value.

See which evidence moved the decision—and which evidence was kept out.
05Grading Intelligence

PSA Intelligence

Model the grade curve, not just the PSA 10.

FlipForge connects sold-comp-backed grade lanes, grading economics, value curves, grade outcomes, and population context where available so the best-case slab price is not mistaken for expected return.

Understand what has to go right—and what happens when it does not.
06Explainability

Decision Traceback

See why the answer changed.

FlipForge preserves the evidence, exclusions, warnings, uncertainty, and reasoning behind the guidance. The result is meant to be traceable rather than a black-box recommendation.

Follow the decision back to the evidence that produced it.
07Learning + Calibration

Outcome Calibration

Compare the decision with what happened next.

FlipForge includes an outcome-calibration layer that can compare prior guidance with resolved outcomes and support calibration review. That creates a controlled feedback loop for improving decision quality without silently rewriting the system or pretending every result was correct.

Deep Dives

Open the intelligence you want to inspect.

The deeper mechanics are available when you want them without turning the whole page into documentation.

01Identity + Evidence TrustWhy the comp is or is not allowed to count

FlipForge keeps seller claims separate from confirmed card attributes. Evidence can be accepted, held for review, or rejected so a wrong parallel, grade, duplicate, or weak record does not quietly become confidence.

02PSA + Grading IntelligenceWhy the PSA 10 price is not the expected return

Raw value, grading cost, sold-comp-backed grade lanes, multiple grade outcomes, break-even points, and downside belong in the same scenario. FlipForge models the range rather than anchoring to one best-case grade.

03Decision TracebackWhy the result changed and what to verify next

The outcome is not meant to be a black box. FlipForge preserves the evidence, exclusions, uncertainty, and reasoning behind the guidance so you can see what caused it and what could change it.
