Private Beta Terms

Experimental access requires honest feedback and independent judgment.

Updated August 15, 2026. Participation is optional, limited, and revocable.

Beta status

The product is unfinished and may contain errors, incomplete data, performance issues, changed features, or unavailable workflows. Beta access is not a promise of public release.

Selection and access

Submitting an application does not guarantee selection. Access may be limited by capacity, device compatibility, testing needs, or conduct. Credentials and beta materials may not be shared without permission.

Beta and paid plans

Private Beta participation does not automatically convert to Scout, Collector, Pro, Founding Pro, or any paid subscription. FlipForge will not create a paid subscription merely because you participate in the beta. Any future paid enrollment requires a separate explicit checkout or other clear acceptance step presented to you at that time.

Beta participation does not guarantee a permanent entitlement, permanent discount, or future price. If a Founding Pro offer is made available, it is a separate optional paid offer with its own stated price and duration; accepting Private Beta access alone does not enroll you in that offer.

Decision support only

Beta outcomes are experimental. Participants must independently verify identity, evidence, seller details, costs, condition, grading assumptions, and the final action.

Feedback

Participants may provide bug reports, workflow observations, screenshots, and suggestions. FlipForge may use that feedback to improve the product without payment or ownership obligation to the participant.

Confidential and proprietary material

Nonpublic builds, internal documentation, unreleased features, and proprietary workflows should not be publicly redistributed without written permission.

Data and removal

Participants should avoid entering sensitive personal information unrelated to product testing. FlipForge may end access, request removal of a build, or change the beta at any time.

No compensation or warranty

Unless separately agreed in writing, beta participation is unpaid. The beta is provided as available and without warranties to the fullest extent permitted by law.
