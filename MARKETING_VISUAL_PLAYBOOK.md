# FlipForge Marketing Visual Playbook

Status: **ACTIVE GUIDANCE**

This document translates earlier marketing concepts into the current FlipForge brand and product truth. The goal is to preserve strong composition ideas while removing off-brand identity, unsupported claims, and generic sports-card-investing language.

## Core positioning

FlipForge is **CARD INTELLIGENCE**.

Primary message hierarchy:

1. `FLIPFORGE™`
2. `Before You Buy, Know Why.`
3. `Signal. Confidence. Advantage.`

Primary supporting statement:

> FlipForge checks the card, challenges the evidence, and explains the decision before you spend.

Use the phrase **“The Bloomberg of sports cards”** only as an explicit vision statement, not as a claim of current market scale or feature parity.

## Visual system

Use only the locked brand direction for production marketing:

- Black `#000000`
- White `#FFFFFF`
- Silver `#8B8F98`
- Gold `#D4AF37`
- Deep Gold `#B8860B`
- Approved cube-in-corners mark
- Söhne when available; repository fallback stack otherwise

Do not carry forward lime green, neon blue/purple, crypto-style glows, alternate F marks, or generic AI-brain branding from old concepts.

## How to reuse the earlier concepts

### 1. Cinematic sports-card hero concept

**Keep:**
- Large slab/card presence
- Strong left/right composition
- Product screen or decision card beside the collectible
- A clear single headline

**Change:**
- Replace green branding with locked black/gold/white
- Replace “sports card market intelligence” with `CARD INTELLIGENCE`
- Replace generic ROI/profit claims with evidence, confidence, risk, and decision state
- Use actual FlipForge product screenshots wherever possible

**Best use:** Homepage hero, paid social landing page, launch graphic.

### 2. “The hobby is broken” problem-page concept

**Keep:**
- Problem-first storytelling
- Step-by-step disconnected workflow
- Strong visual contrast between pain and solution

**Rewrite around FlipForge:**
- Wrong card / wrong parallel / wrong grade
- Weak or mismatched evidence
- False precision from averages or ROI
- Missing decision traceback

Avoid attacking named competitors. Show the category problem instead.

**Best use:** Homepage problem section, pitch deck, founder carousel.

### 3. Dashboard concepts

**Keep:**
- Dense but readable command-center feel
- Strong hierarchy
- Evidence and decision status at a glance
- Chart + card + explanation composition

**Do not use as product screenshots.** They are composition references only. Marketing should display the real SaaS UI after product polish.

**Best use:** Reference for screenshot framing, callout placement, device mockups.

### 4. Community & education concept

The layout is reusable, but the current content is not ready for public claims.

Do **not** publish fabricated:
- member counts
- discussion counts
- testimonials
- country counts
- review stars
- free-trial language

Reframe later as a **Learning / Evidence Lab** section featuring real educational content, decision frameworks, grading analysis, and community discussion only after those capabilities exist.

**Best use:** Future resource center, newsletter hub, education page.

### 5. “Future of sports card intelligence” concept

The flow itself is strong.

Use this FlipForge version:

`DATA → EVIDENCE → DECISION → OUTCOME → CALIBRATION`

This is more accurate than generic `DATA → AI → PROFIT` messaging and directly supports FlipForge's 7 / 14 / 30 evidence program.

**Best use:** Vision section, deck close, About page.

## Signature marketing graphics to maintain

Build and reuse these six visual families:

1. **Hero Decision Card** — one card, one decision, visible why.
2. **Marketplace vs FlipForge** — what the listing shows vs what FlipForge tests.
3. **Intelligence Stack** — Identity → Evidence → Economics → Risk/Confidence → Decision.
4. **Why This Decision** — accepted evidence, rejected evidence, uncertainty, next check.
5. **PSA Scenario** — raw value, grading cost, grade outcomes, expected value, downside.
6. **7 / 14 / 30 Proof Loop** — saved decision → checkpoints → outcome → calibration.

## Claims policy

Do not publish a numeric performance claim unless it is supported by governed evidence.

Examples that require real proof before publication:

- `50K+ opportunities analyzed`
- `90%+ accuracy`
- `24/7 market monitoring`
- guaranteed profit / ROI
- “best” or “most advanced” category claims
- fake testimonials or user counts

Allowed today:

- explain the workflow
- show illustrative examples clearly labeled as illustrative
- describe actual live product capabilities
- show current beta boundaries
- describe the 7 / 14 / 30 validation method

## Website architecture

Recommended public-site sequence:

1. Hero — `Before You Buy, Know Why.`
2. The problem — price without decision context
3. What FlipForge sees — the intelligence stack
4. Interactive demo — identity → evidence → economics → guidance
5. Before vs. After FlipForge
6. Real product screens
7. Identity checker
8. Decision tools / grading scenario
9. 7 / 14 / 30 proof loop
10. Direct comparison
11. Pricing / beta access
12. Vision — `The Bloomberg of sports cards`
13. Final CTA

## Rule for future design work

Do not redesign the product to match an old marketing mockup.

Instead:

> Preserve useful composition → apply locked FlipForge branding → replace generic finance claims with real card intelligence → use the real product as proof.
