## Position
**PRICE IS NOT PROOF.**
A price is an observation. Its usefulness depends on exact identity, source, outcome, recency, depth, and economic risk.
**Before you buy. Know Why.**

Use "reported" and "qualified" accurately. Do not imply FlipForge itself independently verifies cash settlement where the original marketplace evidence doesn't prove settlement. Do not label a low-population card as liquid, or imply any example is a real transaction.

## Audience entry points
- **Collector — wrong match:** “Looks like the same card. Is it the same parallel?” Point to Evidence Lab wrong-parallel lesson.
- **Flipper — margin and execution:** “A $50 spread isn't $50 profit.” Point to Decision Intelligence example, then Beta application.
- **Grading-focused buyer — downside:** “The PSA 10 price isn't a guarantee.” Point to Evidence Lab grade-assumption lesson.

## Proposed short-form posts (not published)
All examples below are conceptual. Use existing FlipForge brand graphics and tightly set type; no need for new card artwork.

**Short 01 — The Wrong Comp (12–15 seconds)**
Hook, 0–3: “One card. Two prices. Not the same parallel.”
3–8: Large quote slides away; reveal *Parallel mismatch → exact comparison excluded.*
8–12: “A price without exact identity can mislead.”
12–15: FLIPFORGE. “Before you buy. Know Why.”
Caption: “Same player doesn't always mean the same card. A wrong parallel can make a deal look better than the evidence supports. Explore the six common comp mistakes in Evidence Lab: https://goflipforge.com/learn.html#busted-comp  #SportsCards #CardCollecting #FlipForge”

**Short 02 — The One-Sale Trap (12–15 seconds)**
Hook, 0–3: “One big sale. One big assumption?”
3–10: *One reported sale* → *Limited sample* → *No guaranteed resale.*
10–15: “Ask what the rest of the market supports.”
Caption: “An extraordinary result can be legitimate and still fail to describe a repeatable market. Learn the evidence rule before your next purchase: https://goflipforge.com/learn.html#busted-comp #SportsCardInvesting #FlipForge”

**Short 03 — ASK Isn't SOLD (12–15 seconds)**
Hook, 0–3: “Listed for $900. Sold for…?”
3–10: Screen reads *ASK ≠ COMPLETED SALE*; seller price stays in context lane.
10–15: “Before you buy. Know Why.”
Caption: “Active listings show seller expectations, not completed-sale outcomes. Understand what a comp needs to prove: https://goflipforge.com/learn.html#busted-comp #CardFlipping #SportsCards”

Do not attach a specific company's name to accusations of misreporting or manipulated sales. Link original industry documentation when explaining sourcing.
