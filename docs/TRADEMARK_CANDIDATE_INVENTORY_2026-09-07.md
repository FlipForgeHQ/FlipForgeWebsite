# FlipForge Trademark Candidate Inventory — 2026-09-07

## Purpose

Create one controlled list of FlipForge names, feature names, and slogans that may deserve trademark protection. This is an internal product/brand inventory, not a legal clearance opinion or a representation that any mark is federally registered.

## Symbol rule

- `™` may be used to signal a claim to a trademark even before a federal application or registration.
- Do **not** use `®` unless and until the USPTO has registered the mark for the applicable goods/services.
- Public use of a symbol does not replace a clearance search or federal filing strategy.

USPTO references:
- https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/basics/what-trademark
- https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/search/federal-trademark-searching
- https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/trademark-fee-information

## Immediate implementation standard

1. Use `Card Decision Intelligence™` on the first prominent textual use on marquee public pages.
2. Keep the actual locked logo asset unchanged until a separately approved logo/brand-package revision is made.
3. Avoid cluttering every occurrence with `™`; first prominent use plus a controlled trademark notice is sufficient for brand presentation.
4. Preserve `FlipForge™` in the copyright/footer treatment.
5. Do not apply `™` broadly to Tier 2 terms until clearance review has begun.
6. Never use `®` before registration.

## Filing workstream

For each Tier 1 mark:

1. Confirm exact owner/legal entity and current use in commerce.
2. Collect first-use evidence and specimens (website, app, launch/beta materials, dated screenshots, invoices/commerce evidence where applicable).
3. Run a comprehensive clearance search: USPTO federal database plus common-law/web/domain/business usage.
4. Identify the exact goods/services and filing basis.
5. Review international classes and use the USPTO ID Manual where accurate.
6. File only after clearance and ownership are confirmed.

Current USPTO base application filing fee is generally $350 per class, with possible additional fees depending on application completeness, custom identifications, and filing basis. Verify the current fee schedule again immediately before filing.

## Product-governance note

Trademark protection should follow durable customer-facing product identity. Do not file every internal technical label. Prioritize the names that customers will repeatedly associate with FlipForge as the source of the product or feature.
