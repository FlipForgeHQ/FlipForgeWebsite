# Instagram templates (founder types and posts these by hand)

Rules for every post:
- Use the brand language: **FLIPFORGE · CARD DECISION INTELLIGENCE · Before you buy. Know Why.**
- Use the decision states **BUY / WATCH / VERIFY / PASS**.
- Never claim guaranteed profit, guaranteed returns, accuracy percentages, "can't lose", or that FlipForge buys,
  sells or executes. This is the forbidden-claims list in `marketing/content/disclaimers.json`.
- Use synthetic or illustrative cards in creative. Do not show a real listing's price, title or seller.
- Keep one link per post, built in `link-builder-and-tracker.html`, with its own `utm_content` tag.

## Captions

**Reel — "Why VERIFY beats a guess"** (`utm_content=reel-verify-explainer`)
> Not every card has enough matching sales to price it honestly. When the evidence is thin, FlipForge says VERIFY
> instead of guessing. Before you buy. Know Why.
> Private beta: link in bio. Decision support only; no guaranteed outcomes.

**Carousel — "Max Buy is a ceiling, not a target"** (`utm_content=carousel-max-buy`)
> Slide 1: The ask looks cheap. Is it?
> Slide 2: Supported Value comes from qualified completed sales of the exact card.
> Slide 3: Max Buy is the highest all-in price the evidence still supports. A ceiling, not an offer price.
> Slide 4: Above Max Buy → WATCH or PASS. Thin evidence → VERIFY.
> Slide 5: Before you buy. Know Why. Private beta: link in bio.

**Story — beta call** (`utm_content=story-beta-call`)
> Testing FlipForge with collectors who'll use it on real decisions and tell us where it's wrong. Apply → (link sticker)

## Comment replies (public, typed by the founder)

- "Good question: VERIFY means there isn't enough matching evidence yet. It doesn't say the card is fake."

## If someone messages you first

You may reply by hand. Do not start DMs, do not send bulk messages and do not use any automation tool.

> "Thanks for reaching out! The private beta application is at the link in our bio. Please apply there rather than
> sending details here. We only use what you put in the form."

Do not ask for their email, phone number or any other detail in DMs. Do not copy their handle into any FlipForge file.

## Perks and disclosure

If a beta perk is offered for sharing or reviewing, the invitation must say so. Any tester post about FlipForge must
then disclose it (e.g. `#ad`, or "I got free beta access"), under FTC endorsement guides. Giveaways need separate
founder approval (N6) and official rules. They must also follow Instagram's promotion guidelines, including a
release of Instagram from responsibility.
