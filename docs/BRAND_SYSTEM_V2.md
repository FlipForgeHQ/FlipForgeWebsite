# FlipForge™ Perfected Brand System

Status: approved and locked

## Governing identity

- Brand: **FLIPFORGE™**
- Identity line: **CARD DECISION INTELLIGENCE**
- Core mark: four open white/silver corner forms around one centered three-dimensional gold cube
- Official slogan: **Before you buy. Know Why.**
- Meaning: noise → discovery → validation → advantage

## Approved palette

- White: `#FFFFFF`
- Silver: `#8B8F98`
- Gold: `#D4AF37`
- Deep Gold: `#B8860B`
- Black: `#000000`

## Website implementation

- `assets/brand/flipforge-logo-horizontal.svg` is the primary website lockup and includes the official slogan.
- `assets/brand/flipforge-logo-stacked.svg` is the approved stacked lockup.
- `assets/brand/flipforge-mark.svg` is the compact icon-only mark.
- `assets/brand/flipforge-app-icon-dark.svg` is the approved favicon and installable web-app tile.
- `assets/css/brand-v2.css` preserves mark scale, lockup spacing, wordmark tracking, identity-line tracking, and responsive behavior.
- `scripts/build-assets.js` injects and validates the perfected brand layer on every root website page during the Netlify build.
- `site.webmanifest` uses the approved app icon.

## Locked rules

1. Keep the three-dimensional gold cube centered.
2. Do not add an outer box around the primary transparent mark.
3. Do not alter the four-corner proportions.
4. Maintain clear space equal to at least the height of the gold cube.
5. Use the icon-only mark at small sizes.
6. Keep **CARD DECISION INTELLIGENCE** uppercase with wide tracking.
7. Preserve the trademark after **FLIPFORGE™**.
8. Keep **Before you buy. Know Why.** in the primary website lockup.
9. Do not restore the deprecated `Signal. Confidence. Advantage.` website tagline.
10. Treat **CARD INTELLIGENCE** and **CARD VALUE INTELLIGENCE** as retired brand descriptors. Product-module or historical uses may remain only when they are not presented as the FlipForge identity line.

## Boundaries

This change is visual branding only. It does not modify Smart Opportunity authority, PSA grading guidance, evidence handling, tenant isolation, authentication, billing, providers, transactions, staging activation, or production traffic.
