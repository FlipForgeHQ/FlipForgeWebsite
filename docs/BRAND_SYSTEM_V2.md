# FlipForge™ Perfected Brand System

Status: approved and locked

## Governing identity

- Brand: **FLIPFORGE™**
- Identity line: **CARD DECISION INTELLIGENCE**
- Core mark: four open white/silver corner forms around one centered three-dimensional gold cube
- Official slogan: **Before you buy. Know Why.**
- Core product promise: **A decision you can not only trust, but understand.**
- Meaning: noise → discovery → validation → advantage

## Positioning lock

FlipForge must not present itself as a card price checker, comp list, charting app, or another market-price dashboard.

The core distinction is:

- **Price is an input. Value is the intelligence.**
- Price data can show what cards sold for.
- FlipForge verifies the exact card, qualifies the evidence, tests the economics, and shows what the evidence actually supports.
- The output is a clear, explainable next move: **BUY, WATCH, VERIFY, or PASS**.
- FlipForge should be associated with evidence-backed **card value intelligence** delivered through **Card Decision Intelligence™**.

The phrase **card value intelligence** may be used descriptively in product and marketing copy to explain the value proposition. It is not a replacement identity line, logo descriptor, or alternate product name. The formal brand identity remains **CARD DECISION INTELLIGENCE**.

## Messaging hierarchy

### Primary attention hook

**The price is easy. Knowing whether to trust it isn’t.**

### Supporting promise

**FlipForge checks the exact card, challenges the evidence, and tests the economics—so you get a decision you can not only trust, but understand.**

### Supporting proof language

- **Exact card.**
- **Qualified evidence.**
- **Supported value.**
- **Clear decision.**

### Value-intelligence distinction

**Price is an input. Value is the intelligence.**

When contrasting FlipForge with simple price data, preferred framing is:

- **Price check:** what similar cards sold for.
- **FlipForge:** what this card’s qualified evidence supports.

Avoid leading with charts, historical sale lists, raw averages, or price lookup as the product definition. These may support a decision, but they are not the FlipForge product promise.

## Video and motion standard

Product films should demonstrate the system rather than explain every internal layer.

Preferred short-film sequence:

1. A card or listing enters.
2. FlipForge confirms the exact identity.
3. Weak or mismatched evidence is rejected.
4. Supported value changes.
5. FlipForge returns a clear decision such as **VERIFY**.
6. The film closes on the approved FlipForge brand lockup and **Before you buy. Know Why.**

Every FlipForge product video must end with:

**FLIPFORGE™**  
**Before you buy. Know Why.**

Because **CARD DECISION INTELLIGENCE** is already incorporated into the approved logo lockup, do not add a competing or redundant closing descriptor around the logo.

Motion should feel precise, premium, and product-led: interface elements resolve, evidence is accepted or rejected, supported value recalculates, and the decision locks. Avoid generic stock motion, fake sci-fi scanning effects, decorative chart walls, or visual noise that makes FlipForge resemble a price-tracking app.

## Approved visual composition

- Deep black foundation with restrained white, silver, champagne-gold, and deep-gold accents.
- Use the approved FlipForge logo and mark without reinterpretation.
- Real product UI and decision evidence should be the primary visual subject.
- Use depth, light, and motion sparingly to create a premium precision-instrument feel.
- Keep copy concise enough that the product interaction carries the explanation.
- Avoid generic SaaS card grids, clip-art, decorative price charts, and invented product metrics.
- Visual hierarchy should move from **card → evidence → supported value → decision**, not from price charts to more price charts.

## Locked rules

1. Keep the three-dimensional gold cube centered.
2. Do not add an outer box around the primary transparent mark.
3. Do not alter the four-corner proportions.
4. Maintain clear space equal to at least the height of the gold cube.
5. Use the icon-only mark at small sizes.
6. Keep **CARD DECISION INTELLIGENCE** uppercase with wide tracking.
7. Preserve the trademark after **FLIPFORGE™**.
8. Keep **Before you buy. Know Why.** in the primary website lockup.
9. Keep **A decision you can not only trust, but understand.** as the core product promise.
10. Keep **The price is easy. Knowing whether to trust it isn’t.** as the approved primary attention hook unless a later approved campaign explicitly replaces it.
11. Preserve **Price is an input. Value is the intelligence.** as the approved price-vs-value positioning line.
12. Every FlipForge product video must close with the approved FlipForge lockup and **Before you buy. Know Why.**
13. Do not restore the deprecated `Signal. Confidence. Advantage.` website tagline.
14. Treat **CARD INTELLIGENCE** and formal **CARD VALUE INTELLIGENCE** as retired brand descriptors. Descriptive lowercase **card value intelligence** may be used to explain the product promise, but not as a replacement identity line.
15. Do not position FlipForge as a price checker, graphing product, or comp-list product. Price data supports the system; it does not define the system.

## Boundaries

These brand rules govern messaging, visual identity, homepage positioning, product-film composition, and marketing presentation. They do not modify Smart Opportunity authority, PSA grading guidance, evidence handling, tenant isolation, authentication, billing, providers, transactions, staging activation, or production traffic.
