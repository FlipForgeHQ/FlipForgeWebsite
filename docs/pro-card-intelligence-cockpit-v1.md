# Pro Card Intelligence Cockpit V1

This customer-facing cockpit composes existing server-owned FlipForge intelligence without creating a second authority.

## Customer view
A saved opportunity detail may show:
- Smart Opportunity BUY/WATCH/VERIFY/PASS decision
- Forge Heat when the server-owned Pro ranking currently surfaces the opportunity
- supported value and saved all-in ask
- confidence, liquidity, and risk
- exact accepted completed-sale evidence
- saved PSA intelligence
- Decision Traceback and evidence chain

## Authority boundaries
- Smart Opportunity remains the sole recommendation authority.
- Forge Heat only ranks eligible saved opportunities.
- The browser never computes, infers, or substitutes a Heat score.
- A missing or gated Heat score is displayed as withheld/not surfaced rather than estimated.
- No view can accept evidence, predict a grade, bid, buy, sell, or authorize a transaction.

## V1 Heat scope
Forge Heat V1 is limited to the tenant's saved evaluated universe. It does not claim continuous market-wide scanning.

## Consumer principle
The customer sees one decision screen while the underlying authorities remain separate and governed.
