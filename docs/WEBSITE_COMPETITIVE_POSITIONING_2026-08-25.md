# FlipForge Website Competitive Positioning — 2026-08-25

## Purpose

This document governs the public website's current market position. It turns the company strategy into a stable customer-facing hierarchy without changing product authority or creating unsupported performance claims.

## Locked brand architecture

- Brand: FLIPFORGE
- Category: CARD INTELLIGENCE
- Promise: Before you buy. Know Why.
- Named enemy: False Confidence
- Core buying question: Is the premium actually supported?

Do not change CARD INTELLIGENCE to CARD VALUE INTELLIGENCE. Price is an input to FlipForge, not the product boundary.

## Market position

FlipForge should not compete by claiming the largest comp database, the fastest free lookup, dealer operations, or generic AI verdicts.

The public differentiation is the decision-evidence layer:

1. resolve exact identity;
2. qualify which evidence deserves to count;
3. preserve exclusions and uncertainty;
4. connect supported value, liquidity, grade economics, confidence, and risk;
5. issue explainable guidance;
6. preserve decision traceback;
7. review outcomes at Day 7 / 14 / 30 for governed calibration.

The website may compare product categories generically (comp lookup, market database/analytics, dealer operations, FlipForge) but should not publish unsupported claims about named competitors.

## Initial GTM wedge

The platform remains broad sports-card Card Intelligence. The first concentrated beta/content wedge is narrower:

**Grade-premium decision support for individual collectors, beginning with modern football rookie autos and scarce parallels.**

This wedge belongs in beta recruitment, examples, education, and content. It should not rename the company or imply the product only supports football.

## False Confidence framework

The reusable public education system is:

- Wrong identity / parallel
- Stale or mismatched comp
- Thin evidence
- Grade-premium assumption

The claim discipline remains process-based. FlipForge may explain that a precise number can be insufficient or misleading for a specific decision. It must not guarantee profit, grade, price, or outcome.

## Busted Comp content system

Busted Comp is the recurring educational franchise. Each case should follow:

1. apparent bargain;
2. evidence problem;
3. False Confidence category;
4. what FlipForge would verify or exclude;
5. decision boundary;
6. Before you buy. Know Why.

## Website hierarchy

Homepage:
- specific illustrative buying decision;
- False Confidence;
- market-fit/category comparison;
- evidence qualification;
- product proof;
- explainable decision;
- outcome accountability;
- beta CTA.

Product:
- exact identity;
- evidence trust;
- grade-premium economics;
- risk/confidence;
- traceback;
- tracking/outcomes.

Evidence Lab:
- teach the decision framework;
- Busted Comp anatomy;
- grade-premium reasoning;
- 7/14/30 calibration.

Private Beta:
- priority cohort messaging for collectors who regularly evaluate modern football rookie autos, numbered parallels, and grade premiums;
- preserve broad eligibility when useful;
- recruit for difficult decisions and useful feedback, not vanity signup volume.

## Architecture boundary

Presentation/marketing only. The website must not duplicate recommendation, identity, evidence, PSA, persistence, tenant, billing, provider, or transaction authority already owned by the canonical application/core.
