# FlipForge Website Restructure — Customer-First Premium Experience

## Objective

Restructure the public website around a single progression: desire first, proof second, depth on demand.

The redesign must make the customer the protagonist and keep FlipForge's evidence-governed intelligence underneath the experience rather than forcing visitors to absorb product architecture before they care.

## Public journey

1. Hero: one tension, one promise, one action.
2. False Confidence: name the problem in the customer's language.
3. Decision Theatre: show how apparent confidence collapses when evidence is challenged.
4. Proof: accepted vs excluded evidence and governed outcome accountability.
5. Activation: challenge one real decision.
6. Beta CTA: bring the card that makes you hesitate.

## Page roles

- Homepage: persuade, demonstrate, convert. Keep deep methodology off the page.
- Product: answer five customer questions — exact card, relevant comps, supported premium, downside, next move.
- Evidence Lab: own the False Confidence / Busted Comp category and teach the framework.
- Launch Plans: explain future levels of decision depth without acting like a live pricing checkout.
- Private Beta: sell the customer benefit first, then qualify the tester in two clear stages.
- App Preview: keep core workflow primary and advanced analysis progressive; staging remains hidden.

## Design rules

- Preserve `CARD INTELLIGENCE` and `Before you buy. Know Why.` exactly.
- No public accuracy percentage until governed outcome evidence authorizes it.
- No transaction authority or profit guarantee.
- Smart Opportunity remains the sole BUY/WATCH/VERIFY/PASS authority.
- Existing PSA Intelligence remains the sole grading-guidance authority.
- Motion must explain a decision, never decorate for its own sake.
- Mobile is its own conversion experience, not compressed desktop.
- Every page gets one dominant CTA.
