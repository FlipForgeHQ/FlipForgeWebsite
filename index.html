About

Why FlipForge exists.

FlipForge was built after returning to a sports-card market that had become fragmented across marketplaces, grading companies, parallel variations, price references, and inconsistent data.

The goal is straightforward: help users identify the correct card, understand the quality of the available evidence, and make a more disciplined decision before spending money.

FlipForge LLC is building the platform as a private-beta decision engine for collectors, flippers, dealers, and investors who want more than a raw price lookup.

Workflow

Discovery to decision, without hiding uncertainty.

Discover

Search active opportunities and establish a standardized card identity.

Support

Attach sold-comp evidence and market context where access is authorized.

Score

Evaluate confidence, opportunity, liquidity, PSA context, and risk.

Review

Trace the decision, record the outcome, and improve future recommendations.

Data Use

Controlled data use. No scraping shortcuts.

FlipForge uses authorized APIs, user-provided evidence, manual validation, and internal application records. The platform does not claim unsupported access to restricted sold-market data.

Active listings are treated as discovery signals, not exact-match value. Historical evidence is displayed only when legally and technically available, and uncertainty is communicated directly.

Compliance

Decision support, not a promise of profit.

FlipForge does not guarantee profit, predict future prices, provide financial advice, purchase cards automatically, or replace independent judgment.

Users remain responsible for validating card identity, condition, grading, seller reputation, transaction costs, and current market conditions before acting.
