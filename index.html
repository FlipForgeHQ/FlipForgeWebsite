How Max Buy is calculated
  1. Supported resale$500
  2. Selling fees, 12%−$60
  3. Shipping and supplies−$15
  4. Your target margin−$65
  5. Max Buy$360

Profit at a given price is $500 − $60 − $15 − the asking price. Return divides that profit by the asking price. Taxes and other costs lower the result. When the card’s identity or its evidence is unresolved, FlipForge returns VERIFY at any price.
