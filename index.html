The Platform

A decision layer between market data and your money.

Sports-card research is scattered across marketplaces, pricing tools, grading databases, auction platforms, population reports, and individual listings. FlipForge organizes that uncertainty into one controlled workflow.

FlipForge does not hide weak evidence behind a confident-looking number.

It helps determine whether the card is identified correctly, whether the evidence is genuinely comparable, whether the asking price is supported, how much confidence the recommendation deserves, and what must be verified before acting.

The questions FlipForge answers

  • Is this the exact card?
  • Are the completed sales truly comparable?
  • What is the true all-in acquisition cost?
  • Is the opportunity priced below supported evidence?
  • What identity, evidence, liquidity, or market risks remain?

What FlipForge is not

  • Not a marketplace, price guide, buying bot, or checkout tool.
  • Not a seller-messaging or bidding system.
  • Not a guarantee of profit or future value.
  • Not a raw resale product for restricted third-party data.
  • Not a substitute for inspecting the card, slab, listing, or seller.
How It Works

Discovery to decision in four controlled steps.

1. DiscoverSearch using natural collector language and review active candidates from connected authorized sources.
2. Confirm IdentityReview the player, year, manufacturer, product, card number, grade, and parallel before treating anything as an exact match.
3. Validate EvidenceAdd or review trusted completed-sale evidence through authorized providers, approved imports, or user-validated records.
4. Decide and TrackEvaluate supported value, all-in cost, confidence, liquidity, and risk before reviewing guidance.

The Intelligence Model

One workflow. Multiple quality gates.

Identity QualityIs the card identified precisely enough to compare?
Evidence QualityAre completed sales exact, recent, trustworthy, and sufficiently complete?
Market PositionIs the opportunity priced below, near, or above supported evidence?
LiquidityIs there enough market activity to support a realistic entry and exit?
ConfidenceHow much trust should be placed in the recommendation?
RiskWhat identity, evidence, condition, pricing, or market issues could invalidate the opportunity?
Data Use

Authorized data. User-provided evidence. No scraping shortcuts.

FlipForge uses a controlled combination of authorized APIs, licensed or approved data feeds, user-entered evidence, approved CSV imports, internal application records, and manual identity and evidence validation.

Active listings are used for discovery and asking-price context. Completed-sale evidence is used only when it is legally and technically available through an authorized source or supplied and validated by the user.

Reference pricing, predictive estimates, asking prices, and verified completed sales remain conceptually separate.

About Us

Built by a collector to solve the problem behind the price.

FlipForge LLC is building a sports-card decision-intelligence platform for collectors who need more than another number. Our purpose is to make the logic behind a card decision visible: confirm the exact card, validate the evidence, explain uncertainty, and help the collector decide what to do next.

Our Mission

Modern collectors can find endless listings, estimates, charts, and opinions. The harder problem is knowing which information deserves to influence a decision. FlipForge was created to turn fragmented data into a disciplined, traceable process.

The challenge is not finding more information. It is determining which information deserves to be trusted.

We are building FlipForge around four permanent principles: surface the right opportunity, preserve evidence, validate identity and quality, and produce an outcome the collector can understand.

TH
Todd Holbein
Founder · Educator · Builder

Todd grew up collecting sports cards throughout the 1990s. When he returned to the hobby in 2023, he found a market filled with online marketplaces, grading companies, parallels, auction platforms, pricing tools, and disconnected data sources.

Researching one card often required several websites. Similar cards appeared under inconsistent names. Asking prices were frequently shown beside completed sales without clearly explaining the difference.

Drawing from his background in education, technology, programming, and systems development, Todd created FlipForge™ to make complex card-market information understandable, evidence-aware, and actionable.
