Evidence Trust

Not every comp deserves equal weight.

FlipForge separates accepted evidence, review-only records, and rejected mismatches so weak or incorrect data does not distort the decision.

Accepted EvidenceExact completed sales and strong comparable results support value.
Needs ReviewAsking prices and incomplete records provide context, not automatic proof.
Rejected MatchWrong parallels, grades, duplicates, and stale records stay out.

About Us

Built by a collector to solve the problem behind the price.

FlipForge LLC is building a sports-card decision-intelligence platform for collectors who need the logic behind a decision—not just another number.

Our Mission

Modern collectors can find endless listings, estimates, charts, and opinions. The harder problem is knowing which information deserves to influence a decision.

The challenge is not finding more information. It is determining which information deserves to be trusted.

FlipForge is built around four permanent principles: surface the opportunity, preserve the evidence, validate identity and quality, and produce an outcome the collector can understand.

Data and Product Boundaries

Authorized sources. Clear uncertainty. No scraping shortcuts.

FlipForge uses authorized APIs, licensed or approved feeds, user-entered evidence, approved imports, and internal records. Active listings are discovery and asking-price context. Completed sales support value only when legally and technically available through an authorized source or supplied and validated by the user.

FlipForge does not guarantee returns, predict future prices with certainty, buy cards automatically, or replace independent judgment. A recommendation is decision support—not transaction authorization.
