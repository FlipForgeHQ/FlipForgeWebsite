The Platform

A decision layer between market data and your money.

Sports-card research is scattered across marketplaces, pricing tools, grading databases, auction platforms, population reports, and individual listings. FlipForge organizes that uncertainty into one controlled workflow.

FlipForge does not hide weak evidence behind a confident-looking number.

It helps determine whether the card is identified correctly, whether the evidence is genuinely comparable, whether the asking price is supported, how much confidence the recommendation deserves, and what must be verified before acting.

The questions FlipForge answers

  • Is this the exact card?
  • Are the completed sales truly comparable?
  • What is the true all-in acquisition cost?
  • Is the opportunity priced below supported evidence?
  • What identity, evidence, liquidity, or market risks remain?

What FlipForge is not

  • Not a marketplace, price guide, buying bot, or checkout tool.
  • Not a seller-messaging or bidding system.
  • Not a guarantee of profit or future value.
  • Not a raw resale product for restricted third-party data.
  • Not a substitute for inspecting the card, slab, listing, or seller.
How It Works

Discovery to decision in four controlled steps.

1. DiscoverSearch using natural collector language and review active candidates from currently connected authorized sources.
2. Confirm IdentityReview the player, year, manufacturer, product, card number, grade, and parallel before treating anything as an exact match.
3. Validate EvidenceAdd or review trusted completed-sale evidence through authorized providers, approved imports, or user-validated records.
4. Decide and TrackEvaluate supported value, all-in cost, confidence, liquidity, and risk before reviewing BUY, WATCH, VERIFY, or PASS guidance.

Data Use

Authorized data. User-provided evidence. No scraping shortcuts.

FlipForge uses a controlled combination of authorized APIs, licensed or approved data feeds, user-entered evidence, approved CSV imports, internal application records, and manual identity and evidence validation.

Active listings are used for discovery and current asking-price context. Completed-sale evidence is used only when it is legally and technically available through an authorized source or supplied and validated by the user.

Reference pricing, predictive estimates, active asking prices, and verified completed sales are kept conceptually separate.

Provider-Independent CoreExternal data providers support the engine. They do not own the final decision logic.
FlipForge Owns the Decision LayerEvidence validation, exact-match controls, confidence calibration, risk, supported value, maximum buy, recommendation logic, traceback, and outcomes remain inside FlipForge.

About the Founder

Why FlipForge exists.

FlipForge was built from the same confusion modern collectors face: too many sources, too many conflicting numbers, and no clear way to separate signal from noise.

Like many collectors, Todd Holbein grew up collecting sports cards throughout the 1990s. When he returned to the hobby in 2023, he quickly realized how much had changed. Familiar price guides had been replaced by online marketplaces, grading companies, parallel variations, auction platforms, pricing tools, and disconnected data sources.

Researching one card often required several websites. Similar cards appeared under inconsistent names. Asking prices were frequently shown beside completed sales without clearly explaining the difference. Different platforms produced different values with little explanation of how those values were supported.

The challenge was not finding more information. It was determining which information deserved to be trusted.

Drawing from his background in education, technology, programming, and systems development, Todd created FlipForge™ to turn fragmented sports-card information into a clearer, evidence-aware decision process.

FlipForge is being built to help collectors, flippers, dealers, and investors discover opportunities, confirm identity, validate evidence, understand uncertainty, and make more disciplined decisions.
