Evidence Trust

A good advisor challenges the comps.

FlipForge separates accepted evidence, review-only records, and rejected mismatches so weak or incorrect data does not distort the guidance.

Accepted EvidenceExact completed sales and strong comparable results support value.
Needs ReviewAsking prices and incomplete records provide context, not automatic proof.

The Advisor’s Questions

The questions FlipForge asks before guiding a purchase.

Is this the exact card?Confirm year, set, number, parallel, variation, grader, and grade.
Is the evidence truly comparable?Accept, review, or reject records based on identity and evidence quality.
Is the asking price supported?Compare total acquisition cost against validated market evidence.
Is there enough liquidity?Consider whether the market can realistically support an entry and exit.
Should the card be graded?Compare grading cost and probability-weighted outcomes against raw value.
What should happen next?Receive explainable BUY, WATCH, VERIFY, PASS, or GRADE guidance.

Data and Product Boundaries

Software-based guidance. Clear uncertainty. No scraping shortcuts.

FlipForge uses authorized APIs, licensed or approved feeds, user-entered evidence, approved imports, and internal records. Active listings are discovery and asking-price context. Completed sales support value only when legally and technically available through an authorized source or supplied and validated by the user.

FlipForge is software-based decision support—not a human financial advisor. It does not guarantee returns, predict future prices with certainty, buy cards automatically, or replace independent judgment. Guidance is not transaction authorization.
