What FlipForge preserves

Accepted

Exact card, grader, grade, and completed-sale status match the target.

Review

Incomplete listing or asking price can provide context but not automatic proof.

Rejected

Wrong parallel, grade, variation, duplicate, or stale record stays out.

Traceback

The final outcome records what was included, excluded, uncertain, and still needs review.

Decision States

Clear guidance without pretending uncertainty is gone.

FlipForge uses candidate and review language—not automatic instructions to spend money.

Buy candidate

Evidence supports consideration

The economics appear favorable, but condition, seller, and current-market checks still matter.

Watch / Verify

More proof is needed

The opportunity may improve, or an identity, evidence, confidence, or price issue must be resolved.

Pass

Current evidence does not support action

The price, risk, mismatch, or weak support makes the card unsuitable under present assumptions.
