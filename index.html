Flippers · Protect the margin

Find the margin before the mistake.

The decision

“Is this actually a profitable opportunity after the costs and uncertainty?”

The problem

An apparent spread disappears fast when comps are wrong, liquidity is weak, or costs are ignored.

The FlipForge framework Decision signals for flippers

  1. 1
    Lock the exact card

    Exact parallel and grade, so the spread is measured against the card you would actually resell.

  2. 2
    Keep the prices that count Key signal

    Only qualified sales count. Asking prices and stale sales are set aside, and a thin market is flagged as liquidity risk.

  3. 3
    Know your Max Buy Key signal

    Max Buy after your selling fees, shipping and target margin, and the cushion between it and the asking price. A break-even price for cards you own.

  4. 4
    Get the decision, and the why

    PASS when the margin is not there. WATCH when the cushion is thin.

How FlipForge helps

A spread only exists after costs, and only while buyers are still there. FlipForge shows both before you commit capital.
