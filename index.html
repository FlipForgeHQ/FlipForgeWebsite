WHY THIS DECISION? See the assumptions + uncertainty

Not live data: Illustrative resale assumption $500. Selling fees 12% ($60), fulfillment $15 and target profit cushion $65. Max Buy = $500 − $60 − $15 − $65 = $360.

Potential profit = $500 − $60 − $15 − asking price. ROI uses asking price as its denominator; additional acquisition costs or taxes would reduce the result.

Evidence and identity: The normal demonstration assumes an exact match and sufficient qualified evidence. Selecting unresolved evidence forces VERIFY at any price, because a low asking price cannot fix uncertain identity or poor source quality.

Demo rule: BUY only at least $25 below Max Buy with resolved inputs; WATCH within $20 above or $25 below; PASS more than $20 above; VERIFY when evidence is unresolved. Actual FlipForge decisions apply broader governed rules and may differ.
