The moat

The difference is not more data. It is disciplined judgment.

FlipForge is designed to keep one exciting price, one weak comparison or one market story from becoming the whole decision.

01 · Identity first

Know exactly what is being evaluated.

Year, set, card number, parallel and grade must line up before evidence deserves influence.

02 · Evidence quality

Not every comparison gets a vote.

Mismatches, duplicates and weak comparisons can remain visible without being allowed to create false confidence.

03 · Context together

Value is considered with uncertainty.

Supported value, evidence strength, liquidity, grading context and risk are viewed together rather than as isolated numbers.

04 · Explain the call

The result has to survive a “why?”

BUY, WATCH, VERIFY or PASS is paired with the strongest reasons so the collector can understand what changed the decision.

What stays private: FlipForge does not publish its proprietary weighting, formulas, thresholds or internal methods. The public exhibit demonstrates the reasoning categories and customer experience—not the blueprint behind them.
Decision accountability

A decision should be remembered—and tested against what happened next.

FlipForge preserves the original decision and its evidence context so later market outcomes can be reviewed against the call instead of disappearing into hindsight.
