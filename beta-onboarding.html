Before your first session

Prepare one useful test card.

You do not need a spreadsheet or a collection export. One clearly identified card and one real decision is enough.

Exact identityKnow the specific card

Use year, set, player, card number, parallel or insert, grader, and grade when you know them.

Real decisionUse a card you genuinely care about

The beta is most useful when you are deciding whether a real opportunity deserves your attention.

Evidence boundaryLet FlipForge reject weak evidence

An active listing can start discovery, but it is not a completed sale. VERIFY is a valid result when the evidence is not strong enough.

Outcome evidence

What to record at each checkpoint.

The original decision stays fixed. Later evidence is compared with it rather than rewritten into hindsight.

Day 0Preserve the decision

Keep the original recommendation, supported value, confidence, risk, and reason trail.

Day 7Check early movement

Record listing status, new evidence, meaningful price movement, and unresolved identity questions.

Day 14Test the original logic

Note whether the evidence strengthened, weakened, or left the original state unchanged.

Day 30Classify the outcome

Compare the later outcome with the original decision without rewriting the original call.
