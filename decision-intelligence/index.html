THE CORE IDEA

Data tells you what happened. Decision Intelligence helps you understand what it means now.

FlipForge does not try to replace collector judgment. It organizes the evidence around the actual decision so the call becomes easier to defend — and remains yours to make.

Data without direction is noise.

Decision Intelligence is the layer between raw market information and action: deciding what belongs, what is weak, what could make the conclusion wrong, and what the evidence currently supports.

2

3

Make the uncertainty visible

ONE DECISION LANGUAGE

Four states. One consistent way to think about the next move.

FlipForge uses the same decision vocabulary throughout the customer experience. It is decision support, not transaction authority.

BUY

The evidence currently supports the purchase at the evaluated price, within the stated risks and limits.

WATCH

The opportunity is interesting, but the price or evidence needs to improve before stronger support is justified.

VERIFY

Important evidence or identity uncertainty still needs to be resolved before confidence is justified.

PASS

The current price, evidence, risk, or combination of factors does not support moving forward now.

WHAT DECISION INTELLIGENCE IS NOT

Clarity without pretending to know the future.

That boundary matters. FlipForge is built to improve the quality of a decision, not to create false certainty.

Not a grade predictor.

Grading intelligence evaluates economics, probability scenarios, and downside. It does not guarantee a PSA grade.

Not a future-price guarantee.

Current evidence and market context can inform a decision without claiming to know where a card's price will go next.

Not an auto-trading system.

FlipForge does not buy, bid, pay, accept offers, or list cards.

Not a replacement for judgment.

The platform explains what the evidence supports. The collector still owns the final call.
