Product Boundaries

What the current build does—and refuses to pretend it does.

FlipForge Does

  • Search real saved SQLite evidence and known candidates
  • Clearly label local evidence, sample snapshots, and new candidates
  • Track manual and authorized sold-comp evidence
  • Separate active asking prices from validated market support
  • Score confidence, liquidity, opportunity, and risk
  • Explain decisions with traceback and audit context
  • Add PSA population and grade context where evidence exists

FlipForge Does Not

  • Present local search as live marketplace search
  • Scrape protected marketplaces or grading services
  • Create fake comps or population records
  • Guarantee profit or future card value
  • Automatically purchase, bid, message, or list cards
  • Treat BUY_READY_CANDIDATE as final financial advice
  • Replace the user’s independent judgment
