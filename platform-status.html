Opportunity Review

PSA Intelligence Foundation

Population data becomes decision context.

The PSA intelligence track adds structured grading and population context to the evidence workflow. It is designed to help users understand supply, grade scarcity, movement, and grade-to-value relationships—not predict future prices.

Population

Population Foundation

Stores reviewed PSA population observations and links them to normalized card identities and evidence records.

Scarcity

Grade & Scarcity Context

Evaluates grade distribution, scarcity signals, population risk, and the difference between raw population counts and useful decision context.

Movement

Movement & Reconciliation

Tracks population movement observations, status history, reconciliation decisions, and unresolved evidence conflicts.

Value Curve

Grade-to-Value Review

Supports reviewed grade-spread and value-curve context while keeping price claims tied to actual evidence.

Decision Packet

Attached Intelligence

PSA observations can be attached to the decision packet so users can see how population context affected confidence and risk.

Validation

Validated Build Bundles

PSA foundation, movement, reconciliation, resolution-status, and decision-context validation runners have passed their current build checks.

Important: FlipForge does not scrape PSA, invent population data, or treat population movement as a guaranteed price signal. PSA intelligence remains evidence context inside the broader decision workflow.
Product Boundaries

What the current build does—and refuses to pretend it does.

FlipForge Does

  • Organize sports-card opportunities and evidence
  • Track manual and authorized sold-comp evidence
  • Separate active asking prices from validated market support
  • Score confidence, liquidity, opportunity, and risk
  • Explain decisions with traceback and audit context
  • Support review outcomes and future calibration
  • Add PSA population and grade context where evidence exists

FlipForge Does Not

  • Scrape protected marketplaces or grading services
  • Create fake comps or population records
  • Guarantee profit or future card value
  • Automatically purchase, bid, message, or list cards
  • Treat a BUY_READY_CANDIDATE state as final financial advice
  • Resell raw third-party data as a standalone feed
  • Replace the user's independent judgment
