## Primary product framing
FlipForge is a **sports-card decision intelligence platform**.

It helps users:
1. resolve exact card identity,
2. challenge the evidence,
3. qualify supported value,
4. expose risk and uncertainty,
5. explain the decision,
6. preserve outcomes for later review.

FlipForge is **not** positioned as:
- a marketplace,
- an auto-buying system,
- a price-only tool,
- a guaranteed-profit product,
- a speculative prediction engine.

## Public decision language
Use:
- BUY_READY_CANDIDATE
- WATCH
- VERIFY
- PASS

Do not invent substitute verdict names without explicit approval.

## Working rule
If a proposed change is not required to complete the user's request, do not make it.

For small brand corrections, preserve:
- layout
- hierarchy
- colors
- spacing
- typography
- wording
- iconography
- functionality

unless the user explicitly asks for those elements to change.
