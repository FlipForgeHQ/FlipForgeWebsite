# Gemini Master Brand Instructions — FlipForge

Use these instructions as persistent workspace guidance.

## Identity
Brand name: **FlipForge**
Descriptor: **CARD DECISION INTELLIGENCE**
Tagline: **Before you buy. Know Why.**

## Brand personality
FlipForge should feel:
- premium
- precise
- evidence-driven
- calm
- credible
- modern
- deliberate
- technically sophisticated without being confusing

Avoid:
- hype
- casino/gambling language
- exaggerated profit claims
- meme-style financial language
- clutter
- generic "AI-powered" filler
- childish sports-card aesthetics

## Visual system
Primary background: black / near-black.
Primary emphasis: gold.
Primary text: white.
Secondary text: silver / gray.

Approved palette:
- White: #FFFFFF
- Silver: #8B8F98
- Gold: #D4AF37
- Deep Gold: #B8860B
- Black: #000000
- Near Black UI: #03070D

Primary type family:
**Geist Sans**

Headings: Bold / Black
Subheads: Semibold
Body: Regular
Accents / highlights: Gold

## Logo rules
Use only approved FlipForge logo assets.

Do not:
- redraw the logo,
- change the cube geometry,
- change bracket geometry,
- change colors,
- rotate the mark,
- stretch the logo,
- alter tagline punctuation,
- use the retired descriptor CARD INTELLIGENCE,
- create a new "close enough" logo.

Current descriptor under the wordmark:
**CARD DECISION INTELLIGENCE**

## Customer-facing language
Prefer:
- exact card
- evidence
- supported value
- confidence
- uncertainty
- risk
- decision
- reasoning
- outcome
- verify

Avoid unsupported claims such as:
- guaranteed
- always accurate
- unbeatable
- certain profit
- guaranteed ROI
- perfect comps
- automatic winner

## Product philosophy
Identity before valuation.
Not every sale is a comp.
Uncertainty is information.
The reasoning must remain inspectable.
The original decision should be preserved for outcome review.

## When editing existing pages
1. Preserve current page purpose.
2. Preserve locked brand language.
3. Keep existing approved assets.
4. Avoid changing unrelated sections.
5. Do not add new product promises.
6. Do not add transaction authority.
7. Do not make accuracy claims unless explicitly authorized.
8. Keep mobile usability equal to desktop usability.
9. Keep public copy simple enough for a first-time collector to understand.
10. Prefer clear hierarchy over dense information.
