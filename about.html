About FlipForge

Built because more data did not make the decision easier.

The hobby has marketplaces, price histories, population reports, analytics, and endless opinions. FlipForge exists to answer the harder question: which evidence deserves to influence the decision—and why?

Todd Holbein

A lifelong collector who returned to the hobby and found a market with more information than ever—but no dependable way to connect that information into a disciplined decision.

Founder principle: a useful system should not simply produce an answer. It should show the reasoning, expose uncertainty, and make the next step understandable.
Why it started

The missing layer was not another price.

After returning to sports cards, Todd could search marketplaces, pricing tools, grading data, auction results, and community opinions. Yet the same questions kept surviving every search:

01

Is this the exact card?Card number, parallel, variation, grader, and grade can change the market entirely.

02

Are these comps truly comparable?A completed sale can still be the wrong evidence for the target card.

03

Is the price actually supported?Asking prices and market noise should not be confused with evidence-backed value.

04

Does grading make financial sense?Best-case PSA 10 value is not the same thing as probability-weighted economics.

05

What would change the decision?A recommendation without visible uncertainty and traceback is difficult to trust or improve.

FlipForge grew from that gap: an evidence-first workflow connecting discovery, exact-card identity, comparable-sale validation, grading economics, confidence, liquidity, risk, decision traceback, and outcome review.

Product Principles

What FlipForge will not compromise.

These are product boundaries, not marketing footnotes.

01

Collector first

Answer the practical questions that matter before someone spends money, sells a card, or pays grading fees.

02

Evidence before action

Weak support, incomplete identity, and market uncertainty stay visible instead of being disguised as certainty.

03

Explain the outcome

Show what entered, what was excluded, what drove the state, and what could change it.

04

No scraping shortcuts

Use authorized APIs, approved feeds, user evidence, and controlled imports rather than building trust on prohibited data collection.

05

No automatic buying

FlipForge does not bid, purchase, or create transaction authority for the customer.

06

Improve through outcomes

A recommendation should be reviewable later so the system can learn where its reasoning held and where it needs calibration.

Proof Philosophy

Don't claim accuracy. Build the evidence to measure it.

FlipForge's 7 / 14 / 30 review process is designed to compare the saved reasoning with what happens next.

The review loop

Every checkpoint asks whether the original evidence, value support, confidence, liquidity, and risk still justify the saved decision.

The standard

No fabricated win rate. No invented paper-trade accuracy. No market-wide monitoring claim unless the product and evidence actually support it. Performance claims should come from governed outcomes, not marketing imagination.

Our Vision

The Bloomberg of sports cards.

The vision is not to imitate a financial terminal's appearance. It is to bring the same expectation of connected information, disciplined context, transparent risk, saved reasoning, and measurable outcomes to sports-card decisions.

Connected card intelligenceDiscovery, identity, evidence, grading, risk, tracking, and outcomes in one system.
Defensible decisionsA user should be able to see why the recommendation exists and what could invalidate it.
Measured learningOutcome review should improve calibration instead of turning every past decision into a forgotten screenshot.
