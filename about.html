About FlipForge

Built to become the decision layer a returning collector could not find.

Modern collectors can find endless listings, estimates, charts, and opinions. The harder problem is deciding which information deserves to influence a purchase, sale, or grading submission.

Our mission

FlipForge LLC is building an evidence-aware decision-intelligence platform that helps collectors slow down, verify the exact card, inspect the support, and understand the risk before acting.

The goal is not to replace judgment. It is to make the decision process visible, disciplined, and repeatable.

Founder

Todd Holbein

Founder · Educator · Builder

Todd collected throughout the 1990s and returned to the hobby in 2023. He encountered disconnected marketplaces, grading companies, parallels, auction platforms, and pricing tools—but no controlled workflow that connected evidence to an explainable next step.

Drawing from education, technology, programming, and systems development, he began building FlipForge.

Product Principles

What FlipForge will not compromise.

These boundaries are part of the product—not fine print added afterward.

Collector first

Answer the practical questions collectors face before they spend, sell, or grade.

Evidence before action

Never disguise weak support, incomplete identity, or market uncertainty as certainty.

Explain the outcome

Show the evidence, exclusions, warnings, and assumptions behind the candidate state.

No scraping shortcuts

Use authorized APIs, approved feeds, user evidence, and controlled imports.

No automatic buying

FlipForge does not bid, purchase, or authorize a transaction for the user.

Improve through outcomes

Preserve later results so the system can be reviewed and calibrated.

A strong product needs skeptical testers.

The private beta is designed to expose weak assumptions and confusing guidance before public launch.
