FlipForge is building Card Decision Intelligence™: credible information, transparent evidence, realistic economics, and an understandable view of risk, brought together before you commit capital.

Meet the Founder

Todd HolbeinFounder, FlipForge LLC

My connection to sports cards goes back to the 1990s. When I returned to collecting in 2023, I discovered a hobby with more information, marketplaces, grading options, and pricing tools than ever before. Yet finding a price and knowing whether a card was actually a good purchase were still two very different things.

I found myself moving between websites, comparing cards that looked nearly identical, questioning sales records, and trying to understand what the numbers really meant. The information existed in pieces, but the reasoning behind a good decision was often missing.

Drawing from my teaching experience and background in technology and programming, I started building FlipForge to solve that problem. As an educator, I’ve always believed that understanding why something works matters more than simply getting an answer. I wanted to bring that same principle to collecting.

FlipForge is built around a straightforward belief: a price alone should never determine a buying decision. The exact card matters. The quality of the evidence matters. Risk, liquidity, grading, fees, and uncertainty matter.

My goal is to build the most trusted decision-intelligence resource possible for sports-card collectors, flippers, and dealers. Not another price guide, but a platform that helps people understand what the evidence supports, what remains uncertain, and why a particular decision makes sense.

That trust has to be earned through transparency, accuracy, and accountability—not marketing claims.

Before you buy. Know Why.

A professional standard for a market that runs on guesswork.

Serious financial markets rely on information systems held to a demanding standard: documented sources, clear provenance, and tools professionals consult before they act. Sports-card buyers deserve the same discipline. Prices are easy to find; knowing whether to trust them is not.

That is the standard FlipForge is building toward. Our aim is to become the most trusted sports-card information and decision-intelligence platform in the industry. It is an aim, not a claim. Trust of that kind is earned slowly, decision by decision, and we intend to earn it in the open.

Five commitments, in order.

  1. Authoritative information

    Verified card identities, dependable data sources, documented provenance, appropriate market coverage, and fresh information come first. The exact card has to be confirmed before price gets a vote.

  2. Earned trust

    Assumptions and limitations stay visible. Questionable comparisons are rejected rather than quietly counted. Where the evidence is thin, FlipForge says so instead of inventing confidence.

  3. Decision intelligence

    Identity, completed-sale evidence, liquidity, economics, and risk are connected into governed BUY, WATCH, VERIFY, and PASS outcomes. Every decision comes with its reasoning, so it can be understood and challenged.

  4. Professional reliability

    A dependable platform: strong security, tenant isolation, monitoring, verified backups, controlled releases, and a consistent experience wherever you use it.

  5. Value worth paying for

    We focus on capabilities that solve meaningful problems for collectors, flippers, and dealers, and that justify a sustainable subscription rather than a novelty.

Credibility is mostly about what you refuse to do.

  • We do not manufacture transactions, valuations, or evidence.
  • We do not count an asking price as a completed sale, and when we find a mistake in our evidence, we correct it.
  • We do not let a weak or mismatched comparison strengthen a decision.
  • We do not promise profits, perfect accuracy, or complete market coverage.
  • We do not buy, bid, or sell on your behalf. The decision, and the transaction, stay yours.

Measured over time, not asserted up front.

A saved decision keeps the evidence and reasoning that existed when it was made. FlipForge then reviews what actually happened, so the original call can be tested honestly instead of rewritten later.

Our long-term objective

Simple to state and hard to achieve: to become the resource serious sports-card market participants routinely consult before committing capital.
