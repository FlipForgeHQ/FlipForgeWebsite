WHY FLIPFORGE EXISTS

More data did not make the decision easier.

The hobby already has marketplaces, price histories, population reports and opinions. FlipForge exists for the harder question: which evidence deserves to influence your decision—and why?

Customer firstYour decision is the point.

The system exists to help the collector understand what matters before spending.

Evidence before confidenceUnknown stays unknown.

Incomplete identity and weak support should not be disguised as certainty.

Outcomes over claimsMeasure what happens next.

Trust should come from governed evidence and later review—not invented accuracy.

The origin

The missing layer was not another price.

Founder Todd Holbein returned to a hobby with more information than ever and kept running into the same unanswered questions.

01

Is this the exact card?

Card number, parallel, variation, grader and grade can change the market completely.

02

Are these comps truly comparable?

A completed sale can still be the wrong evidence for the card in front of you.

03

Is the premium actually supported?

Asking prices, best-case grades and scarcity stories should not quietly become proof.

04

What would change the decision?

A recommendation is more useful when the uncertainty, exclusions and next check remain visible.

Non-negotiables

What FlipForge will not compromise.

These are product boundaries, not marketing footnotes.

Evidence

Weak support stays visible.

Questionable records are not made safer by averaging them into good evidence.

Explainability

The reason trail survives the verdict.

You should be able to inspect what entered, what was excluded and what could change the answer.

Authority

No automatic buying.

FlipForge does not bid, purchase or create transaction authority for the customer.

Learning

Improve through outcomes.

The original decision stays fixed so later evidence can test the reasoning instead of rewriting history.

Proof philosophy

Don't claim accuracy. Build the evidence to measure it.

FlipForge uses 7 / 14 / 30 outcome review to compare the saved reasoning with what happens next. No fabricated win rate. No public accuracy percentage before governed evidence supports it.

Day 7Early signal

The vision

Connected Card Intelligence—not another dashboard full of numbers.

The long-term goal is a system where discovery, identity, evidence, grading, risk, tracking and outcomes work together so collectors can make decisions they can actually defend.
