Short version: FlipForge is an authenticated web-based card-intelligence workspace. It is not a marketplace, not an auto-buy tool, and not a guarantee of price, grade, or profit.

No. Pricing evidence is one input. FlipForge is designed to validate exact identity, classify evidence quality, evaluate economics, confidence, liquidity, grading context, and risk, then explain the resulting decision state.

FlipForge uses four decision-support states: BUY, WATCH, VERIFY, and PASS. BUY means the saved evidence supports the purchase at the evaluated price, within the stated risks and limits. It is still decision support—not transaction authority—and it does not guarantee a return.

BUY means the saved evidence supports the purchase at the evaluated price within the stated risks and limits. WATCH means there may be an opportunity, but the price or evidence is not strong enough yet. VERIFY means there is not enough trustworthy evidence yet to make the purchase confidently. PASS means the current evidence does not justify the purchase at the evaluated price.
