READ THE NUMBERS CORRECTLY

Three different things must stay separate

A later price move is not the same thing as a good original decision. Three layers stay separate:

01 · Raw outcome signal

What happened later?

A later move can trigger review. It does not decide whether the original call was wrong.

02 · Economic interpretation

Was BUY actually justified at T0?

Outcome evidence is reconnected to the original price, profitability gate, margin of safety, and Maximum Buy Price.

03 · Policy-review candidate

Did economics allow BUY but the recommendation still hold back?

Only a BUY-eligible non-BUY decision becomes a policy-review candidate. No rule changes automatically.

METHOD

The original decision stays frozen

The same frozen decision is followed through time. Later evidence adds context; it never rewrites T0.

T0

Exact identity, qualified evidence, supported value, risk, recommendation, and original economics are preserved.

T7

When sufficient governed outcome evidence exists, FlipForge records an observation and compares it with the frozen T0 decision.

T14

The same study denominator and original decision remain intact. New evidence adds another checkpoint rather than replacing history.

T30

Longer-horizon evidence is added under the same rules, enabling more stable calibration and cross-cohort comparison.

Review

Repeated findings may become a human-reviewed policy hypothesis, but no automatic self-training or production threshold change is authorized by this public proof layer.

WHY THE 17-CASE FINDING MATTERS

A rising card can still have been a poor buy at the original price

All 17 raw conservative signals were checked against authoritative Day-0 profitability. None was BUY-eligible at T0.

15 cases

Negative net economics

The modeled opportunity did not clear the governed profitability requirements at the original acquisition terms.

2 cases

Margin of safety not met

The raw value relationship looked interesting, but the protection required for BUY was not sufficient.

16 cases

Ask above Maximum Buy Price

For 16 of the 17, the original asking price was above the price FlipForge considered economically acceptable under the stored T0 economics.

What this does not mean

It does not prove that FlipForge will always make the right decision, guarantee profit, or establish a universal performance percentage. It means the current raw conservative signals should not be described as 17 missed BUY opportunities.
